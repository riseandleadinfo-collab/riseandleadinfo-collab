Why we exist

Named for an Educator who gave everything.

Sister Marie Bruna Boucard
In memoriam · d. 2010
She devoted her life to education and guidance, so her students could reach their full potential.

Sister Marie Bruna Boucard taught in Bel-Air, a crowded neighborhood of Port-au-Prince. She never had children of her own; her students were her life's work. She saw education as the key to success, the one sure way a child could change the course of their own life.

On January 12, 2010, the elementary and middle school she led collapsed in Haiti's earthquake. She didn't make it out.

A bereavement gift of $1,000 came in her memory. In 2017 her family turned it into a scholarship fund. The first plan was to rebuild what she'd started at her own school. When that school's new leadership couldn't be reached, the mission widened to any bright student held back by money instead of talent.

Every scholarship we award still carries her name.

Who we help

Bright, and in need. That's the whole bar.

Recruited through parochial schools

We partner with parish and parochial schools across Haiti, and in the Dominican Republic, to find students who deserve a shot.

Demonstrated financial need

Scholarships go to families who cannot cover tuition on their own. It is the difference between school and no school.

Academic excellence

Every scholar is chosen for real academic promise, not just circumstance.

Performance keeps the award

Scholars maintain superior performance year after year to stay in the program. It is a partnership, not a handout.
